Help & frequently asked questions.

Learn how quote attribution works, how downloads are created and what happens to your text when you use the site's tools.

Is VERSE & VAULT free?

Yes. The quote library, filters, favorites, card designer, PNG export and copy tools are implemented with standard browser technology. No paid API, account or subscription is required.

How do I download my quote card?

Open the card designer, type your quote, choose your colors and typography, and click Download PNG. The browser draws a high-resolution image using its built-in canvas API and starts a local download. If the browser blocks downloads, check its download permissions.

Does this site use an AI model?

No. The quotes in the library are prepared text, and the design tools use deterministic browser code. There is no AI generation or paid API hidden behind a button.

Are the quotes attributed to famous people?

The sample library contains original text created for this website concept and labels it as original. It does not attribute those lines to famous people. If you add a quotation from another author, verify the wording and source independently before publishing it.

Can I make an Islamic reflection quote card?

Yes. Select Islamic Reflections in the library or type your own reflection into the designer. The included lines are original reflections, not Qur'an verses or authenticated hadith. If you use scripture or a religious narration, verify the exact wording, reference and translation before sharing.

Where are favorites stored?

Favorites are saved in local storage in your current browser, when available. They are not connected to an account and are not synced between devices. Clearing browser data can remove them.

Does the Share button upload my PNG?

No. The browser's native sharing feature may share text or a generated file when supported and permitted. Otherwise, the site copies the text or provides a clear fallback. Nothing is posted to a social network automatically.

Why might a font look different on another device?

The site uses system fonts and common browser fonts instead of loading external font files. The PNG export uses browser canvas text rendering, so exact letter shapes can vary slightly between operating systems.

Does the contact form send messages to a server?

No. There is no backend. The form validates your input and opens a prepared email draft in your email application. You must send the message yourself. You can also use the direct email link in Contact Us.

Can I use my downloaded designs commercially?

The card designer creates a layout from text and styling. You are responsible for having the rights to the quote, names, artwork or other material you enter. This website does not provide a legal guarantee that every use is permitted.
